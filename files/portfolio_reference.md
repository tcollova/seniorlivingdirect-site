## Patterns across the portfolio

### Range of tonal expression
The same agency can produce a refined Dalí-themed editorial ad and a high-energy summer savings flyer. **The voice matches the community and the campaign goal, not a single house style.**

### Real photography over stock
Most pieces feature real residents, real food, real architecture. When stock is used, it's curated to feel candid.

### Senior living-specific design choices
- Type sizing slightly larger than typical print design (audience consideration)
- Generous spacing on event details (RSVP info, dates, addresses)
- Phone numbers and physical addresses always prominent
- Accessibility marks (wheelchair, fair housing) included as standard

### Multi-purpose pieces
Several pieces combine educational content with functional elements (vouchers, RSVP info, calendar layouts). These work harder per dollar of postage.

### Recurring brand devices
For Country Place specifically:
- Watercolor floral logo motif
- Curved white shape transitioning between hero photography and content
- Red/burgundy + dark blue color anchors
- Address bar at the bottom with reversed-out type

## How to use this catalog

When creating new work, reference these as benchmarks:
- **Editorial / sophisticated audience?** → Look at the Legacy pieces
- **Event invitation?** → Donuts and Bunco are templates
- **Promotional / move-in offer?** → Summer Savings and Cornerstone
- **Resident referral or community engagement?** → Countryside referral piece
- **Long-form lifestyle content?** → Country Place dining brochure
