Executive Summary

at a glance

Elbeta Gloves represents a strategic engagement opportunity for JSH & Co. in a high-growth, regulated safety equipment market. The company has genuine manufacturing advantages but lacks the sales infrastructure, distributor management, and marketing execution required to capture meaningful share in California and the Southeastern United States — directly aligned with JSH's core service offering.

01 — MARKET TIMING
Growing, Regulated Market

Industrial safety gloves represent a $12.33B global market expanding 5.2–7.8% annually through 2030. North America commands 35% of demand, with the U.S. alone generating $2.6B in 2024 revenue. Structural tailwinds: OSHA regulation, workplace safety budgets, e-commerce/logistics growth.

02 — COMPETITIVE GAP
Sales Infrastructure Missing

Elbeta has no documented regional sales team, weak digital marketing, and an underdeveloped distributor network. Competitors Ansell (10.5% share), Honeywell, and 3M dominate via 500+ distributor networks and dedicated regional reps — a clear, addressable gap.

03 — GEOGRAPHIC WHITE SPACE
CA + SE Underserved

California ($280–320M) and the Southeast ($400–500M) represent $680–820M in addressable market that Elbeta has not penetrated. Both regions have clear customer segments and distributor recruitment opportunities suited to JSH's expertise.

04 — ENGAGEMENT THESIS
$255K–700K+ Phase 1

Phase 1 scope — California market entry, distributor recruitment, sales structure — represents $50–100K initial engagement scaling to $255–700K within Year 1 across both regions, with Year 2+ potential at $300–500K ongoing.

Recommendation: Pursue a structured Phase 1 engagement focused on California, with discovery-stage scoping ($50–100K, 90 days) and clear success metrics — distributor signed, pipeline developed, sales compensation finalized. Conditional scale to the Southeast follows proof of traction. This is a client worth pursuing — provided JSH structures the engagement conservatively, assesses Elbeta's execution capacity early, and gates expansion on demonstrated results.
