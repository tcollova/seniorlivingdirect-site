Services

Full-service, senior-living focused.

Research, strategy, creative, media, digital, and AI — all under one roof, all tuned specifically for communities serving independent living, assisted living, memory care, skilled nursing, rehab, home health, and hospice.

01

Research & Strategy

Good marketing starts with good information. We do the digging so decisions are grounded in reality, not guesswork.

  • Feasibility research for new builds and expansions
  • Demographic analysis of your primary market area and residents
  • Competitive analysis and positioning
  • Mystery shopping of your community and competitors
  • Prospect and resident surveys (development, administration, analysis)
  • Marketing campaign and budget development
  • ROI and post-campaign analysis
02

Creative Services

Our creative team has spent years developing the visual and verbal language that resonates with seniors and adult children. We know what works, because we've tested it, measured it, and refined it across hundreds of campaigns.

  • Print advertisements and collateral (brochures, catalogs, flyers)
  • Direct mail design and campaign execution
  • Logo and identity systems (letterhead, business cards, folders)
  • Digital assets (banner ads, social media, email)
  • Outdoor and signage
  • Newsletters, menus, calendars, resident packaging
  • Floor plans and employment advertising
  • Copywriting grounded in senior living and gerontology expertise
  • Custom photography of architecture, residents, staff, and events
  • Full-service video: commercials, tours, interviews
  • Full-service audio: commercials and online
03

Media Planning & Buying

We buy media like we're spending our own money, because we're spending yours. Every placement is accountable to a specific goal, and we audit everything.

  • Print media buying, negotiation, and placement
  • Digital: pay-per-click, Facebook, Instagram, and other social platforms
  • Radio buying and production
  • Broadcast and cable television
  • Outdoor and transit
  • Media efficiency and demographic alignment analysis
  • Placement audits to verify you got what you paid for
04

Digital Services

The myth that seniors aren't online is long dead. Boomers and their adult children make life decisions through search engines and social media. We make sure your community shows up.

  • Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram account setup and maintenance
  • Web development and site overhauls
  • Search engine optimization
  • Mobile app development (through trusted partners)
  • Digital communications and full-service blogging
  • Internal communications and press release distribution
  • Editorial content development
05

Media Relations

When your community deserves attention, we help make sure it gets it — through local press, industry publications, and community events.

  • Media list development and management
  • "House of messages" development for consistent storytelling
  • Full-service press release development and distribution
  • Earned media placement in broadcast, print, radio, and online

Custom AI, built for your community.

We build custom AI systems that work the way your community works — trained on your voice, calibrated to your positioning, overseen by our senior living strategists. They handle competitor monitoring, content drafting, lead qualification, creative iteration, and more.
