About Us

We do one thing, and we've been doing it a long time.

Senior Living Direct is a mid-sized ad agency dedicated to serving seniors and the senior living industry across the United States and Canada. We've been at this since 1999.

Our Story

A quarter century in senior living.

Our work is narrow by design. We don't do car dealerships or restaurant groups. We do senior living — all of it. Independent living, assisted living, memory care, skilled nursing, rehab, home health, hospice, CCRCs, personal care homes, adult day programs. We understand the business models, the regulatory environments, the census pressures, and the very specific people who make decisions to move themselves or their parents.

That narrow focus is our biggest advantage. We don't have to learn your industry. We've been in it for twenty-five years.

Our clients are communities of all sizes and budgets, from single-location operators to regional portfolios. We've helped new buildings lease up from zero and helped established communities find residents who are the right fit — not just a signed contract, but someone who belongs there.

What We Believe

How we work, in plain terms.

  • Quality over quantity. A lead is only valuable if it's the right lead. We'd rather send you ten prospects who fit than a hundred who don't.
  • Your voice, not ours. Every community has a personality. Our job is to find it, sharpen it, and make sure everything we produce sounds like you.
  • Strategy before tactics. Pretty ads that don't work aren't pretty. We start with who you need to reach and why — the creative comes from there.
  • Exceptionally responsive. You shouldn't have to chase your agency. We return calls, meet deadlines, and treat your timelines like they're ours.
  • Highly ethical. Seniors and their families deserve marketing that's honest. We don't use fear tactics, dark patterns, or misleading claims. Ever.
  • Easy to work with. No posturing, no jargon walls, no billable-hour games. We're a team that's enjoyable to have on your team.
AI, Practically

We use AI. We don't hide behind it.

AI has changed how agencies work, and we've been early to adopt the tools that genuinely help. Data analysis that used to take a week takes a day. Competitor monitoring that used to be a quarterly exercise happens continuously. Creative iteration is faster.

But AI, on its own, produces generic work. It doesn't know why an adult daughter is anxious about her father's decline, or why a brochure that leads with amenities loses to one that leads with a quiet morning on the porch. That context comes from us.

Our approach is simple: let technology handle the heavy lifting so our strategists can focus on the parts that require judgment, taste, and twenty-five years of senior living experience.
